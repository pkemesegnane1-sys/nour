Chapitre 18

Ce chapitre est un outil de diagnostic. Lisez-le une première fois en entier (30 minutes), puis revenez-y une fois par mois. Chaque erreur est présentée avec sa forme fautive, sa forme correcte et l'explication. Ne travaillez qu'une erreur à la fois : choisissez-en trois par semaine, affichez-les, et corrigez-les dans tous vos écrits et enregistrements jusqu'à l'automatisme.

Erreurs 1 à 25 — le verbe et ses temps

*I have 25 years.I'm 25 (years old).L'âge se dit avec be.
*I have cold / hunger / fear.I'm cold / hungry / afraid.Sensations avec be + adjectif.
*I am agree.I agree.agree est un verbe, pas un adjectif.
*I am knowing him.I know him.Verbes d'état jamais au -ing.
*I want that you come.I want you to come.want + personne + to.
*He don't work.He doesn't work.3e personne : doesn't.
*He doesn't has a car.He doesn't have a car.Après does, base verbale.
*Did you went?Did you go?Après did, base verbale.
*She don't like it.She doesn't like it.Idem 3e personne.
*I have seen him yesterday.I saw him yesterday.Date précise → passé simple.
*I live here since 2015.I've lived here since 2015.« Depuis » → present perfect.
*I am here since Monday.I've been here since Monday.Idem avec be.
*I will call you when I will arrive.I'll call you when I arrive.Présent après when.
*If I will have time…If I have time…Jamais will après if.
*I used to go there when I was young (correct) *I am used to goI'm used to going.be used to + -ing.
*He can to swim.He can swim.Pas de to après un modal.
*She cans / *he musts.She can / he must.Les modaux ne prennent jamais -s.
*I mustn't come (= interdit)I don't have to come.« Pas obligé » → don't have to.
*I have gone to Paris twice.I have been to Paris twice.Expérience → been.
*She's gone to the market (elle est au marché)She's gone to the market.Correct — mais vérifiez le sens voulu.
*I didn't nothing say.I didn't say anything.Une seule négation.
*I don't never go.I never go.never suffit.
*I have make a mistake.I have made a mistake.Participe passé, pas base.
*She has wrote it.She has written it.3e forme.
*I didn't knew.I didn't know.Base après didn't.

Erreurs 51 à 75 — structure de phrase et vocabulaire

✘ Forme fautive✔ Forme correcteExplication
✘ Faux✔ JustePourquoi
*Is cold today.It's cold today.Sujet it obligatoire.
*Have a problem.There's a problem.il y a = there is.
*It has two rooms.It has got / There are two rooms.il y a $≠$ it has.
*I am a Senegalese.I'm Senegalese.Nationalité : adjectif sans article.
*She is a doctor woman.She is a doctor.Pas de redondance.
*I said you something.I told you something.tell + personne, say + chose.
*He explained me the problem.He explained the problem to me.explain n'accepte pas le double complément direct.
*I demand you a question.I'll ask you a question.demand = exiger.
*Make attention!Be careful! / Watch out!Calque de « faire attention ».
*Make a photo.Take a photo.Collocation imposée.
*Do a mistake.Make a mistake.make pour créer, do pour exécuter.
*Do a party.Have / throw a party.Collocation.
*Make sport.Do sport / play sports.Collocation.
*I have a good relation with him.I get on well with him.relation = relation amoureuse ou diplomatique.
*The people is nice here.The people are nice here.people est pluriel.
*I am boring. (je m'ennuie)I'm bored.-ed = ressenti.
*The film was bored.The film was boring.-ing = cause.
*I have 32 years old.I'm 32.Redondance.
*It's difficult for me of understanding.It's difficult for me to understand.for + personne + to.
*I think to go.I'm thinking of going.think of + -ing (projeter).
*I look my phone.I look at my phone.look at ; watch pour ce qui bouge.
*I see TV every night.I watch TV every night.watch = attention prolongée.
*The weather is very hot (correct, mais)It's very hot.Plus naturel.
*How is it called?What is it called?what pour un nom.
*How do you call this?What do you call this?Même règle.
Erreurs 76 à 100 — discours, prononciation, style

✘ Forme fautive✔ Forme correcteExplication
✘ Faux✔ JustePourquoi
*He asked me where did I live.He asked me where I lived.Question indirecte : ordre affirmatif.
*I don't know what does it mean.I don't know what it means.Idem.
*The man who I met him.The man (who) I met.Ne pas répéter le pronom.
*My brother, that lives in Dakar…My brother, who lives in Dakar…that interdit en relative non définissante.
*I am living here since 2010.I have been living here since 2010.Durée → perfect.
*She said me that…She told me that…say ne prend pas de personne directement.
*I am since one hour here.I have been here for an hour.for + durée.
*I search my keys.I'm looking for my keys.search = fouiller un lieu.
*I will see you to tomorrow.I'll see you tomorrow.Préposition inutile.
*Actually I'm working (= actuellement)Currently I'm working.actually = en fait.
*Eventually we'll meet (= éventuellement)We'll possibly meet.eventually = finalement.
*I visited my grandmother (rendre visite)I visited my grandmother. ✔Correct ici — mais visit a place est plus fréquent ; pour les personnes, see est souvent plus naturel.
*I want a coffee (au café)I'd like a coffee, please.Registre : want est brutal.
*Give me the salt. (à table, adulte)Could you pass me the salt, please?Politesse obligatoire entre adultes.
*I have the possibility to come.I can come. / I have the chance to come.possibility = faisabilité technique.
*It is necessary that you come.You need to come. / You must come.Éviter les structures lourdes.
*I suggest you to come.I suggest coming. / I suggest that you come.suggest refuse suggest sb to do.
*He explained me everythingHe explained everything to me.Structure imposée.
*I prefer coffee than tea.I prefer coffee to tea.prefer A to B.
*I am agree with youI agree with you.Déjà vu, mais faute tenace.
*This is more better.This is better.Jamais more + comparatif.
*He runs more fast than me.He runs faster than me.Comparatif en -er.
*I have so much friends.I have so many friends.many + dénombrable.
*There is many people.There are many people.Accord avec le pluriel.
*I look forward to hear from you.I look forward to hearing from you.to est une préposition ici.

Les cinq fautes de prononciation à corriger en premier

  1. Le h avalé : he, him, house, happy — soufflez.
  2. Le th transformé : think $≠$ sink ; this $≠$ dis.
  3. Le w dit v : we $≠$ ve ; water $≠$ vater.
  4. Le e final ajouté : book bouk, pas bou-keu.
  5. L'accent tonique plat : baNAna, inforMAtion, toDAY.

➤ MISSION Chapitre 18 — trois erreurs par semaine
Chaque lundi, choisissez trois erreurs de ce chapitre (pas plus). Écrivez-les sur un papier que vous gardez dans votre poche ou sur votre bureau. Pendant toute la semaine, chaque fois que vous parlez ou écrivez en anglais, vérifiez ces trois points. Le dimanche, faites un test : écrivez 10 phrases en utilisant les trois structures surveillées. En 33 semaines, vous aurez éliminé les 100 erreurs — et personne ne devinera plus que vous êtes francophone en vous lisant.
